## 1. Ethical Photography & Humanitarian Disclosure Policy

SHONGJOG strictly adheres to the Core Humanitarian Standard (CHS) and ethical non-exploitative visual principles:

1. **Contextual Authenticity:** All photographic assets represent genuine physical geography, coastal infrastructure, riverine transit, or community resilience activities within the southwest coastal delta of Bangladesh (or documented accessible transport mechanisms).
2. **Non-Exploitative Representation:** No graphic disaster suffering or distressed victims are depicted. Images convey dignity, structural resilience, and civic cooperation.
3. **Simulation Disclosure:** None of the individuals pictured are represented as actual SHONGJOG beneficiaries or rescue subjects. Photography is used purely to establish authentic geographic, cultural, and technical context for this research simulation.
